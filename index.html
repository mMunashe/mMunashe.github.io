Allipa

1M+

transactions processed

People wanted to buy electricity, airtime, data and everyday goods without installing another app.

Allipa is an AI agent that handles the whole sale in one chat, on the web or in WhatsApp, from choosing a product to paying for it. It has now processed more than a million transactions.

Chat with Allipa Chat on WhatsApp

What you can ask Allipa to do

Allipa now covers far more than airtime. It is also the front door to several of the systems below, so a customer can get an insurance quote, order a DNA test or ask about a pension in the same conversation.

  • Shopping and browsing products
  • Orders, quotes and delivery
  • Events and RSVPs
  • Insurance quotes and applications
  • DNA tests
  • DStv renewals
  • Pensions
  • Money transfers abroad
  • Fuel requests for fleet cards
  • Agent wallet and commission
