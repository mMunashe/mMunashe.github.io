01About

Hukuimwe

Flutter app

Broiler farm management for small-scale farmers

An offline-first mobile app for small-scale broiler farmers in rural Zimbabwe. Its health scanner covers ten diseases, using image analysis together with a symptom-based expert system. It also works out what a batch will need, tracks money in and out, and keeps vaccination schedules.

Flutter, Dart, SQLite
